What We Do

Three offerings. Engagements usually combine them. Most clients start with one and add the others as the work proves out.

Full-stack applications

Net-new web and mobile applications, built end to end on a modern stack and shipped to production. Any language the job calls for.

  • Customer-facing products (B2C)
  • Products you sell to other businesses (B2B)
  • Internal tools and line-of-business systems

AI integration and orchestration

AI wired into the systems you already run, rather than a standalone prototype that never reaches anyone’s daily work.

  • Agents and copilots that work against your data
  • Automations across Slack, Notion, and your existing stack
  • Document processing, extraction, routing, and summarization

AI enablement and training

We set your team up to build this way themselves, using the same tooling we use on the engagement.

  • Claude Code, Codex, and frontier agent tooling
  • Agentic workflow and automation setup
  • Co-working alongside your staff, then handoff

How Engagements Run

Find the problem, build the thing, hand it over.

One fixed fee covers all three phases, run as a sequence rather than a menu.

Phase What happens
01 Audit and discovery An AI-specific audit of your business. We find where AI actually helps you: which products are worth building, which workflows are worth automating. The output points at specific things to build, with an estimate and a sequence.
02 Build We build and ship working software. Not a report, not a prototype. Production code, integrated with your systems, running in your environment.
03 Transfer We co-work with your people throughout the build and hand off ownership at the end. The capability stays in-house after we go.

$22,000 for an 8-month engagement, fixed fee, covering all three phases.

What Makes This Different

We ship, not advise. A strategy firm rarely leaves a working product behind. Every engagement ends with software running in your hands. That is the line between us and a traditional consultancy, and it is deliberate.

Strategy and engineering in one team. The people deciding what to build are the people building it. Nothing is lost in a handoff between a strategy team and a delivery vendor, because there is no handoff.

MBB-caliber thinking, AI-accelerated pricing. Led by an ex-McKinsey faculty advisor, delivered by a bench of MBAs and undergraduate engineers. The work meets a top-tier consulting standard at a fraction of top-tier rates.

We are built on the tooling we sell. We run on frontier agent tooling internally. When we set up your team, we are teaching the workflow we use every day, not one we read about.

What Makes a Strong Project

The best fits share three traits: there is a real business problem behind it, the answer is not already fully specified, and there is something to ship at the end.

Strong fits:

  • A product you want built. Net-new web or mobile application, B2C, B2B, or internal
  • A workflow that should be automated. Document handling, routing, triage, summarization, reporting
  • An agent or copilot over your own data. Something your team would actually open daily
  • AI wired into systems you already run. Not a standalone demo
  • An AI audit. You suspect AI helps somewhere but do not know where, and want a concrete, sequenced list of what to build
  • Enablement. Your team needs to learn to build this way, with your real codebase

Poor fits:

  • Pure research with no artifact at the end
  • Work that requires systems access that cannot be provisioned
  • Safety-, clinical-, or compliance-critical determinations
  • Staff augmentation on an existing backlog
Note

Not sure if you have a project? That is what the discovery audit is for. It is scoped small, fixed fee, and it ends with a concrete list of what to build and what it would take.

Partner Expectations

  • Assign a single point of contact with authority to answer scope questions
  • Hold a standing weekly call of 30 to 45 minutes
  • Provide data and systems access early, and provision any necessary accounts
  • Answer decision requests raised in the weekly update within one week
  • Participate in phase reviews
  • Accept public recognition as a partner, unless anonymity is requested

Expect roughly an hour a week from your point of contact.

Commercial Terms

$22,000 per engagement, fixed fee. A standard engagement runs 8 months and covers all three phases: audit and discovery, build, and transfer. The fee is fixed at the start. There are no hourly rates and no change orders for work inside the agreed scope.

Fee $22,000, fixed
Duration 8 months
Team 2 dedicated builders, plus a QC lead and faculty oversight
Guaranteed capacity 20 team-hours per week, 640 hours over the engagement

The team is small on purpose. Two builders working 10 hours each per week, with QC and faculty oversight riding on top, is enough to ship a focused product and not enough to sprawl. Discovery exists to make sure what we build fits inside that capacity.

A shorter discovery-only engagement is scoped separately and priced smaller, for clients who want to find out what is worth building before committing to build it.

Intellectual property: partners retain full rights to all deliverables, code, models, and documentation produced during the engagement.

Confidentiality: every student signs the partner’s NDA, or the Foundry’s standard mutual NDA, before receiving data. Client data is confined to the approved project repository and approved tools, and local copies are deleted at the end of the engagement.

Timing and Capacity

We accept projects on a rolling basis. There is no application deadline and no single annual intake. Phase boundaries are set per engagement rather than against a university calendar, and a discovery audit can start on a short cycle.

The practical constraint is bench capacity, not the calendar. We run a deliberately limited number of concurrent engagements so every team is staffed and supervised properly. When the bench is full, new engagements queue for the next opening. Tell us your timing and we will tell you honestly when we could start.

How to Start

  1. Send us the problem, or tell us you are not sure yet and want an audit
  2. We review for fit and follow up within one week
  3. A 45-minute scoping call covers the problem, timing, and phase structure
  4. Discovery is scoped and confirmed in writing, with fee, IP, and confidentiality terms
  5. Discovery output determines whether and how build and transfer are scoped
