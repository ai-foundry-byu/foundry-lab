Program Overview

The BYU AI Foundry is an AI-native product studio and consultancy, and an official program of the BYU Marriott School of Business. We build AI products. One team works out what is worth building and then ships it, because those are no longer two jobs.

Start a conversation →

Three Offerings

Engagements usually combine them. Most clients start with one and add the others as the work proves out.

Full-stack applications. Net-new web and mobile applications, built end to end on a modern stack and shipped to production. Any language the job calls for. Customer-facing products, products you sell to other businesses, and internal line-of-business tools.

AI integration and orchestration. AI wired into the systems you already run, rather than a standalone prototype that never reaches anyone’s daily work. Agents and copilots that work against your data, automations across Slack, Notion, and your existing stack, and document processing, extraction, routing, and summarization.

AI enablement and training. We set your team up to build this way themselves, using the same tooling we use on the engagement. Claude Code, Codex, and frontier agent tooling, agentic workflow and automation setup, and co-working alongside your staff before handoff.

Three Phases

Find the problem, build the thing, hand it over. One fixed fee of $22,000 covers all three phases across an 8-month engagement. Run as a sequence rather than a menu.

Phase What happens
01 Audit and discovery An AI-specific audit of your business. We find where AI actually helps you: which products are worth building, which workflows are worth automating. The output points at specific things to build, with an estimate and a sequence.
02 Build We build and ship working software. Not a report, not a prototype. Production code, integrated with your systems, running in your environment.
03 Transfer We co-work with your people throughout the build and hand off ownership at the end. The capability stays in-house after we go.

What Makes This Different

We ship, not advise. A strategy firm rarely leaves a working product behind. Every engagement ends with software running in your hands. That is the line between us and a traditional consultancy, and it is deliberate.

Strategy and engineering in one team. The people deciding what to build are the people building it. Nothing is lost in a handoff between a strategy team and a delivery vendor, because there is no handoff.

MBB-caliber thinking, AI-accelerated pricing. Led by an ex-McKinsey faculty advisor, delivered by a bench of MBAs and undergraduate engineers. The work meets a top-tier consulting standard at a fraction of top-tier rates.

We are built on the tooling we sell. We run on frontier agent tooling internally. When we set up your team, we are teaching the workflow we use every day, not one we read about.

Who Is on Your Team

Each engagement is staffed with a mixed team drawn from the MBA bench and the undergraduate builders. Every team carries three roles:

Role Responsibility
Client lead Your single point of contact. Runs the weekly call, sends the weekly update, owns scope conversations
Builders The engineering. Application code, integrations, models, and deployment
QC lead Reviews everything before it reaches you. Owns the quality standard

Product evaluation and QC is a standing function inside the Foundry, not something invented per project. A faculty advisor oversees every engagement.

Academic Credit

Students enroll in the AI Foundry Lab, cross-listed as MBA 693R and STRAT 490R, for 3.0 credit hours per semester and 6.0 across the year. Academic credit is how the bench is staffed; it does not change what you receive or the terms you receive it under.

Commercial Terms

$22,000 per engagement, fixed fee. A standard engagement runs 8 months and covers all three phases: audit and discovery, build, and transfer. The fee is fixed at the start. There are no hourly rates and no change orders for work inside the agreed scope.

Fee $22,000, fixed
Duration 8 months
Team 2 dedicated builders, plus a QC lead and faculty oversight
Guaranteed capacity 20 team-hours per week, 640 hours over the engagement

The team is small on purpose. Two builders working 10 hours each per week, with QC and faculty oversight riding on top, is enough to ship a focused product and not enough to sprawl. Discovery exists to make sure what we build fits inside that capacity.

A shorter discovery-only engagement is scoped separately and priced smaller.

Intellectual property. You retain full rights to all deliverables, code, models, and documentation.

Confidentiality. Every student signs your NDA, or the Foundry’s standard mutual NDA, before receiving data.

Timing

We accept projects on a rolling basis. There is no application deadline. Phase boundaries are set per engagement, and a discovery audit can start on a short cycle. The constraint is bench capacity, so when the bench is full new engagements queue for the next opening.

What We Do Not Do

  • Pure research with no artifact at the end
  • Work requiring systems access that cannot be provisioned
  • Safety-, clinical-, or compliance-critical determinations
  • Staff augmentation on an existing backlog
