Evaluation

Sponsors complete a brief feedback survey twice: at mid-year for developmental purposes, and at the end of the year as an input into student grades.

Feedback Survey

The survey asks you to evaluate the team on five dimensions, each on a 1 to 7 scale:

  • Timeliness: Meeting deadlines and showing up prepared
  • Communication: Clarity and effectiveness in writing and in meetings
  • High Agency: Taking initiative, solving problems proactively, working independently
  • Technical Competency: Skill in analysis, modeling, and building
  • Contribution: Consistently delivering work of real value

You will also be able to comment on each dimension. Comments are shared with the students.

Survey Due Date Purpose
Mid-year December 11, 2026 Developmental feedback only, no grade impact
End-of-year April 14, 2027 Final evaluation, input to grading

You evaluate the team, not individual students. Individual differentiation comes from confidential peer evaluation within the team, which is our job to administer, not yours.

Mid-Year Feedback

The mid-year survey gives the team a chance to understand where they stand with half the year left. It has no impact on grades. Its purpose is to let them course-correct while there is still time.

After completing it, have a live conversation with the team. Schedule it within a week. This conversation is one of the most valuable parts of the program for students.

Mid-Year Conversation Guide

Tip

Make it two-way. Ask the team for their feedback on the engagement and on what you could do differently as a sponsor. They will have opinions and will be reluctant to volunteer them.

  1. Strengths: What is going well? What should they keep doing?
  2. Growth areas: Where do they need to be sharper by April?
  3. Support: What can you do to help? Data access, introductions, faster decisions?
  4. Scope check: Is the M1 problem statement still the right problem?
  5. Ask for feedback: How can you be a better sponsor?

End-of-Year Feedback

The end-of-year survey is the final evaluation and a graded input. It is shared with students, so please discuss it verbally as part of a wrap-up conversation.

Wrap-Up Conversation Guide

  1. Highlights: What did they deliver that you will actually use?
  2. Growth areas: What skills should they develop before their first job?
  3. Career advice: What would help them succeed in this kind of work?
  4. What happens next: Will you act on the recommendation? Is there a follow-on engagement, an internship, or a hire here?
  5. Ask for their feedback: What could the Foundry do better?

Student Feedback on the Engagement

At the end of the year we also ask students to evaluate the engagement: quality of mentorship, whether the work mattered, clarity of expectations, and access to data and decisions. We share the results with you. It is the most direct read you will get on how to sponsor better next time.
