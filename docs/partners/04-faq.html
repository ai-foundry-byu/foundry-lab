Frequently Asked Questions

Cost and Terms

What does it cost?

Fixed fee, scoped per phase. Discovery is scoped small and deliberately low-commitment. Build and transfer are scoped to the work once discovery has defined it. Each phase can be bought on its own.

We quote a fixed number before a phase starts. There are no hourly rates and no open-ended engagements.

Why phase it rather than quote the whole thing?

Because before discovery, neither of us knows what should be built. Quoting a full build up front means one of us is guessing, and the client absorbs the error either way. Discovery ends with a concrete list of what to build and an estimate, and you decide from there whether to continue.

What does the fee cover?

Faculty oversight, the delivery team’s time, software and API costs, tooling and infrastructure for the engagement, and program administration.

When is payment due?

Payment terms and vendor setup are handled through BYU. Contact us and we will connect you with the right administrative contact early, so vendor onboarding does not delay the start.

Is there a formal agreement?

Yes. Each phase is confirmed in writing before it starts, covering scope, fee, IP, and confidentiality.

Intellectual Property and Confidentiality

Who owns the work?

You do. Clients retain all deliverables and intellectual property, including code, models, documents, and analysis.

Will students sign an NDA?

Yes. Every student on the team signs your NDA, or the Foundry’s standard mutual NDA if you prefer. This happens before any data is shared.

How is our data handled?

Client data stays in the approved project repository and approved tools. It does not go into students’ personal accounts, personal cloud storage, or unapproved AI services. Students delete local copies at the end of the engagement. Data handling terms can be tightened to match your requirements.

Can students talk about the project publicly?

Not without your written permission. That includes LinkedIn, portfolios, and job interviews. The default is that the engagement is confidential. Many clients do grant permission for a general description, which helps students and costs you nothing, but it is your call.

Do you use AI tools on our data?

Yes, and it is governed. We are an AI-native studio; frontier agent tooling is how the work gets done. Students use approved tools only, under your data handling terms. If you have a specific list of permitted or prohibited AI services, tell us at scoping and we will fit the toolchain to it.

Scope and Fit

What makes a strong project?

Something with a real business problem behind it, where the answer is not already fully specified, and where there is something to ship at the end. A product you want built, a workflow that should be automated, an agent over your own data, AI wired into systems you already run, or enablement for your own team.

What if we do not know what we need?

That is what the discovery audit is for. It is scoped small, fixed fee, and it ends with a concrete list of what to build and what it would take. Most clients start there.

Do you deliver production software or a prototype?

Production software. Working code, integrated with your systems, running in your environment. Leaving a working product behind is the line between us and a traditional consultancy.

What is a poor fit?

Pure research with no artifact at the end, work requiring systems access that cannot be provisioned, safety-, clinical-, or compliance-critical determinations, and staff augmentation on an existing backlog.

Can the scope change mid-engagement?

Yes, in writing. Changes get documented in the decision log and confirmed in the weekly update. What we manage against is quiet expansion, where questions are added without anything coming off the plan.

What if the engagement isn’t going well?

Contact the faculty advisor immediately. Most problems are fixable early and expensive later. Early intervention is normal program operation, not an escalation.

Working Arrangements

When can we start?

We accept projects on a rolling basis. There is no application deadline. Tell us your timing and we will tell you honestly when the bench has room.

How much of my time does this take?

About an hour a week: a 30 to 45 minute standing call, plus reading the weekly update and answering the decisions it asks for. Plus phase reviews.

Do we need to be local to Utah?

No. Engagements run remote by default. On-site visits are welcome but not required.

Do we provide equipment or accounts?

Students use their own machines. You will need to provision accounts for data or systems access, which we scope at the start.

Who maintains the software after handoff?

You do, and transfer is designed for exactly that. We co-work with your people throughout the build and hand off ownership at the end, so the capability stays in-house after we go.

Can we hire the students?

Yes, and many clients do. Recruiting access is one of the real benefits of working with us. Please route offers through the faculty advisor during the engagement so it does not disrupt the team.

Can we run a second engagement?

Yes. Most clients start with one offering and add the others as the work proves out.
