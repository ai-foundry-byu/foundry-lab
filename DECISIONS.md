## Deliberate choices worth reviewing

- **Peer evaluation carries individual differentiation, client evaluation is team-level.** This
  protects you from a client who likes the outgoing student and undervalues the quiet one, but
  it means students grade each other. Some faculty hate that.
- **The syllabus has a hard confidentiality section with an Honor Code hook.** Real client data
  and student portfolios collide constantly. Better to make the rule explicit up front.
- **"What We Do Not Do" is on the partner overview page.** Most programs bury this. Saying it
  plainly filters bad-fit inbound before it costs you a call, which matters more once CapSource
  is generating volume.
- **The RFP names the engineering distinction directly.** Your inbound will partly be projects
  engineering declined, exactly like Breckenridge. Making the boundary explicit turns that from
  an awkward rerouting into a clean referral path.
